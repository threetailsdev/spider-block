How hidden images look

Seeing a hidden image

Clicking a hidden image opens a small panel where you can choose to see it.

Finding spiders

Hide images until they've been checked

Pictures may take a moment longer to appear, but you won't catch a glimpse of a spider before it's hidden.

Look for spider words

Checks alt text, captions, file names, titles and links around each image, in more than 40 languages.

Scan images on this device

An image-recognition model built into Spider Block looks at each picture. Images never leave your computer.

Hide every image on pages about spiders

For example a search for spiders, or an encyclopedia article about them.

Some sites don't let extensions download their images.

Extras

Cover spider words in page text

Show how many images were hidden

A small number on the toolbar icon.

Hide everything at once

Press Alt+Shift+H (or right-click and choose Hide or show all images on this page) to instantly hide every image and video on the page. Press it again to bring them back. You can change the shortcut in your browser's extension settings.
