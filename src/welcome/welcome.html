Welcome to Spider Block

From now on, pictures of spiders are hidden as you browse.

One more step: your browser needs your OK before Spider Block can protect websites.

One more step: allow Spider Block on all websites, or Safari asks you on every site. On a Mac, open Safari → Settings → Extensions → Spider Block → Edit Websites and set Other Websites to Allow. On iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, then Apps → Safari → Extensions → Spider Block, and set Other Websites to Allow.

How it works

Reads the words around pictures

Captions, file names, titles and links, in more than 40 languages.

Looks at the picture itself

An image-recognition model runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Holds pictures back until checked

So you don't catch a glimpse of a spider before it's hidden.

Choose how hidden images look

You can change this any time in settings.

Good to know

  • If a spider slips through, right-click it and choose Hide this image. It stays hidden from then on. No filter is perfect, so this matters.
  • Hide everything at once with Alt+Shift+H. Press it again to bring the pictures back.
  • Clicking a hidden image asks before showing it. You can turn showing off entirely in settings.
  • The toolbar button lets you pause Spider Block or turn it off for one site.
  • Spider-Man stays visible unless you change that in settings.
