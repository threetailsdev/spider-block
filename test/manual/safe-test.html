Waiting for the extension…

Massive orb weaver on the porch

When the checklist is green, try these:
  1. Click a hidden tile: a small panel asks whether to show it.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+H: every tile disappears. Press it again to bring them back.
  3. Right-click the beach tile and choose Spider Block → Hide this image. It stays hidden after a reload.
  4. Open the extension's settings. Under Scan images on this device it should say Status: ready, which means the image scanner works in this browser.
